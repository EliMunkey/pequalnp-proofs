# Independent audit of the 18-gate finite exclusion

I read the project evaluator and quantified statement, the signed-AND frontier, the physical AND/OR frontier in ordered.cpp, the restriction and cone headers, the cofactor-bound generator, the 9-vertex and 10-vertex logs, and the exact two-branch Lean reduction. I found no branch that could discard a computing circuit.

* The arbitrary input map can duplicate or omit projections; giving all ten projections is a relaxation. A unary function of x₀ is a constant or one of its two signs. An out-of-range wire supplies false. Empty AND/OR, unary AND/OR, and NOT of up to two listed operands require no charged binary gate in U2. Consequently an 18-gate circuit with at most 17 binary AND/OR gates embeds in the 17-gate U2 test. All remaining gates of a hypothetical exception must be 18 binary AND/OR gates.
* Any live, dead-gate-free representation of that exception uses ten essential primary functions. Even when x₀ and ¬x₀ both appear, they are a single underlying wire offering either polarity on every use. Retain an operand exactly when it will be read again. The total retains are 2m−(n+m−1)=m−n+1. Hence every such circuit has a path through the retirement enumeration. Legally constant gates and boundary-duplicate live wires may be bypassed and redundant branches pruned. When the special wire and its complement meet, the result is a constant.
* The U2 normalization has one representative in every complement pair, selected to be false at zero. Any signed binary AND/OR (including signs of operands or output) is one exceptional two-bit pattern, up to output complement. For two opposite target assignments whose live vectors differ at one candidate source, retiring that source is safe only if the other source equals its exceptional bit. For opposite targets differing in both selected coordinates, retiring both requires the particular pair's parity to equal the exceptional pattern's parity. No other opposite-label pair can newly collide. Thus those tests only discard impossible tails.
* Fixing x₀ makes all pure gates monotone in the other nine coordinates. Agreement on the positive minima and negative maxima of both fibers forces agreement on the entire cube. Taking cross-products of positive and negative boundary inputs for the separator test is valid because an all-monotone suffix needs some live coordinate equal to 1 on the positive input and 0 on the negative; the x₀ input can supply either sign. Directly enumerating the independently listed graph edges gives 38 total rows. In the graph with vertex 0 deleted, a dual rail at an adjacent vertex gives 32 rows, and one at a nonadjacent vertex gives 29. These match the two 15-gate negative logs. A pure circuit with no dual rail after a deletion is a subset of either dual-rail model.
* At a prefix, k−1+spare equals its remaining gate allowance. The sorted pairs of register indices are a complete topological order convention. After any fixed first gate, greedily choose a ready gate minimizing (larger operand index, smaller operand index). A gate newly made ready needs the most recent new register and sorts after every gate previously ready. The exceptions on the first comparison permit freely choosing an orbit representative. The root orbit calculation explicitly fixes x₀ and preserves edges, so its reduction is sound.
* A completed memo key records exact physical boundary truth-table IDs, dual availability, register places, and the permitted pair-order floor. On noninitial prefixes the most recently produced register remains live, so the maximum live place determines depth and future numbering. A tail transplants between matching frontiers with equal functions on every boundary row. It still gives a full correct circuit by fiber monotonicity even if the two prefixes disagree on interior rows. Consequently history-dependent restriction flags, specialized cone shapes, and slacks may differ without invalidating the memo rejection. Every cost-pruning step is necessary for any full correct completion of its own earlier prefix. C++ compares complete keys after hashing. The integer packing has no collision within the observed limits; register places are below 32.
* For a nonconstant pinned target let d bound the number of prefix gates that simplify, and c count live signals proved constant by structural propagation. The suffix must simplify at least c additional gates: every gate reduces the live constant count by at most one more than it preserves, and the final output is nonconstant. The scalar increase in d+c under a transition is its number of retained constant operands plus one if exactly one operand is constant and blocks the other. This agrees with both pinStep borrow masks. For the cone cutoff, rebuilding the union of necessary ancestors of the live restricted signals costs at most their union cardinality. The specialized suffix costs at most the remaining budget minus c. A pure AND/OR gate simplified at a constant keeps the other physical signal or yields a constant, so this rebuilding also preserves the restricted physical gate class.
* When a single zero pin retains x₀, a relabeling of the Petersen graph maps the pair (removed vertex, retained dual vertex) to one of the two ordered distinct-pair orbits (adjacent or nonadjacent). If it removes x₀ instead, the remaining pure unsigned graph falls within the checked relaxation. Hence the ten strength-16 pins in actual/pins-petersen.txt follow from the two smaller searches. Other pins use the previously computed unconditional U2 bounds. The signed 17 root uses the original weaker pin table, avoiding circular reliance on the purely physical 18-branch calculations.
